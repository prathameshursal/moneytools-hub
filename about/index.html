About MoneyTools Hub

MoneyTools Hub makes free, fast, privacy-friendly calculators and plain-English money guides, focused on India.

Why this site exists

Money decisions are stressful, and most tools online make it worse: pages bury a simple calculator under pop-ups, demand sign-ups, or push a product. We wanted the opposite. Every tool here works instantly, works on a slow phone, asks for nothing in return, and never sees your numbers, because every calculation runs in your own browser.

What we make

  • Calculators for the decisions that come up repeatedly: loans, investments, taxes, deposits, and everyday numbers like BMI, age, and percentages.
  • Guides that explain the same topics honestly, including the trade-offs other pages skip. We would rather tell you that an SIP can lose money than pretend it cannot.

Principles

  1. Private by design. No accounts, no tracking of calculator inputs, nothing uploaded. Ads, when shown, are served by Google and governed by our privacy policy.
  2. Honest about limits. Tax rules change, markets fluctuate, and simplified models differ from lender statements. We say so on every page where it matters.
  3. Free, forever. The site is funded by advertising, not by selling your data or recommendations.

Editorial standards

Content is researched from primary sources (Income Tax Act, GST Council communications, RBI notifications) and reviewed before publication. When you spot an error, tell us via the contact page and we will fix it. Nothing on this site is financial, tax, legal, or medical advice; read the full disclaimer.
