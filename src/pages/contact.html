Contact us

Found a bug, spotted an error in a guide, or want to say hello? We read everything.

The button opens your own email app with the message pre-filled, so you keep a copy and we never store anything on this site. You can also write directly to hello@example.com (replace this with your real address in the code and on this page).

What to include

  • Bug reports: the page URL, what you entered, what you expected, and a screenshot if possible.
  • Content corrections: the guide or calculator and the specific line that is wrong. Sources help.
  • Business queries: a one-line description and your timeline.

We usually reply within a few business days. No sales pitches for guest posts on unrelated topics, please.
