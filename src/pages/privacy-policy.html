Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information MoneyTools Hub ("we", "us", "this website") collects when you visit, and the choices you have. We designed the site to collect as little as possible.

Information we collect directly

Almost none. All calculators run entirely in your browser using JavaScript. The numbers you enter (loan amounts, salary, height, dates) are processed on your device and are never transmitted to us, logged, or stored on any server. We do not require accounts, and we do not ask for your name, email, phone number, or payment details to use any tool.

Google AdSense and cookies

This site displays advertising served by Google AdSense to cover hosting costs.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info/choices.
  • Where required by law (including for EEA/UK visitors), a consent banner lets you accept or reject personalised advertising before ads load.

Analytics

We may use privacy-respecting, aggregated traffic analytics to understand which pages are useful. Analytics data is used in aggregate and is not linked to calculator inputs.

Contact form

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and message. We use it only to reply to you and do not add you to any mailing list. We do not sell or share correspondence with third parties except where required by law.

Children's privacy

This site is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data security and retention

Because we do not store calculator inputs or maintain user accounts, there is no user database to breach. Emails you send us are retained only as long as needed to handle your query.

Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (for example under the EU GDPR or India's DPDP Act), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold about you. Since we hold almost none, the practical scope is limited to correspondence you have sent us. To exercise any right, email us via the contact page.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site evolves. Material changes will be reflected by the "last updated" date above. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us through the contact page.
