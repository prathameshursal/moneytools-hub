On top of slab tax, a 4% health and education cess applies. The Section 87A rebate makes tax nil for taxable incomes up to ₹12,00,000 (salary income after the ₹75,000 standard deduction, which corresponds to gross salary of about ₹12,75,000). Marginal relief applies near the threshold, which this simplified estimate does not model.

This is an estimate for planning. Actual liability depends on your exact components (HRA is not deductible in the new regime, but employer NPS is), surcharge above ₹50 lakh, and any changes in Finance Acts. Verify with a CA or on the official portal before filing.

Frequently asked questions

Which is better, new or old regime?

It depends on your deductions. If you claim large amounts under HRA, 80C, home-loan interest, and NPS, the old regime can still win. With few deductions the new regime usually costs less. Compute both before choosing; salaried taxpayers can switch each year unless they have business income.

Is the ₹12 lakh rebate the same as "no tax up to ₹12 lakh"?

For ordinary income, yes: the 87A rebate wipes out slab tax up to ₹12 lakh taxable. Special-rate income like capital gains does not get the rebate.

Why does my employer's TDS differ from this estimate?

Employers annualise from declarations, apply marginal relief, and handle special-rate income differently. Treat this tool as a ballpark.

Does this store my salary?

No. The calculation runs in your browser and nothing is transmitted or saved.
